ChefJiw, a Thai cooking app for iPhone and Android, in Thai or English. All prices are in Thai baht.
Chef Elephant walks you through a dish one step at a time on your phone: what to do now, how high the heat should be, a timer, and what to look for. Big buttons for messy hands, and the screen stays on. Stuck? Tap "Ask the chef" (3 typed questions a day, free).
Free to try: basil pork with fried egg, and green curry from paste, the dish people most often get wrong from a video, so you can see if the chef does better before you pay anything.
The 12-dish pack, 99 baht once, yours to keep, no subscription: 12 more dishes most home cooks can't make from memory (such as tom yum, pad see ew and massaman). Every dish is cooked by home cooks before it goes live, and most need only one pan. If a dish doesn't come out right, tap "It didn't work" and the chef goes through the step with you.
The pack includes 4 Sunday cook-ahead plans: three dishes that keep all week, one shopping list, and what the week costs per meal at market prices, next to buying a 50-baht plate.
More packs come later, each sold separately.
