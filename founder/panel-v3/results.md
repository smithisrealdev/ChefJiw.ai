## What would flip a no

- Let me try one Sunday cook-ahead plan free. If it really feeds my family for less than buying 50-baht plates and works in my small kitchen corner, I would pay the 99 baht.
- Let me cook one full Sunday plan free, with amounts for my whole family, and see it come out right.
- More free dishes to try first, like five instead of two and including one I find hard, so I can see it really comes out better than following a video.
- If one Sunday plan were free, so I could see that a week of cooking for one really costs less than buying 50-baht plates before I pay.
- If I actually cooked the free green curry and it came out clearly better than my YouTube attempts, I would pay the 99 for the pack.
- If I could try one Sunday plan for free and it really fed my family for less than street food, using only my one pan, I would pay the 99.
- Let me try one Sunday cook-ahead plan for free. If the shopping list really comes out cheaper per meal than a 50-baht plate using only my one pan, I would pay the 99.
- Let me try one Sunday cook-ahead plan for free. If my week's meals really come out cheaper than a 50-baht plate, the 99 baht pays for itself and I'd buy.
- If I could ask the chef out loud, hands-free, instead of typing while I cook.
- My mum, my sister or a friend I cook with telling me they used it and the dishes really tasted like home.
- If I actually cook the free green curry and it comes out clearly better than my YouTube tries, I'd pay the 99 baht.
- If I tried the free green curry and it came out clearly better than when I follow a YouTube video, I would pay the 99 baht.
