ChefJiw, a Thai cooking app for iPhone and Android, in Thai or English. All prices are in Thai baht.
Chef Elephant walks you through a dish one step at a time on your phone: what to do now, how high the heat should be, a timer, and what to look for. Big buttons for messy hands, and the screen stays on.
Stuck at a step? Tap "Ask the chef" for the answers people usually need there, or type your own question to the AI chef.
Two dishes so far: basil pork with fried egg (15 minutes, about 50 baht of ingredients) and Thai pork omelet (10 minutes, about 30 baht).
The app is free, including 3 typed questions a day. What is for sale is the membership: unlimited typed questions, 99 baht a month.
