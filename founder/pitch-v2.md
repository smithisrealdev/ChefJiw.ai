ChefJiw, a Thai cooking app for iPhone and Android, in Thai or English. All prices are in Thai baht.
Chef Elephant walks you through a dish one step at a time on your phone: what to do now, how high the heat should be, a timer, and what to look for. Big buttons for messy hands, and the screen stays on. Stuck? Tap "Ask the chef".
Free: two starter dishes (basil pork with fried egg, Thai pork omelet), the step-by-step coach and 3 typed questions a day.
Membership "A new dish every week", 99 baht a month: 12 more dishes at launch that most home cooks can't make from memory (such as green curry from paste, tom yum and pad see ew), then one new dish every week. Every dish is cooked by home cooks before it goes live, and most need only one pan. If a dish doesn't come out right, tap "It didn't work" and the chef goes through the step with you.
Members also get a monthly Sunday cook-ahead plan (three dishes that keep all week, one shopping list), a shopping list with the cost of every dish next to what the same dish costs to buy ready-made, and unlimited typed questions.
First month free, then 99 baht a month. Cancel any time in your app store.
