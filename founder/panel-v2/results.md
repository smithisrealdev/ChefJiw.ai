## What would flip a no

- Seeing, with my own market's prices, that its dishes really feed my family for clearly less than buying plates downstairs, even after paying the 99 baht.
- If the Sunday cook-ahead plan came every week instead of once a month, with one shopping list that shows I really spend less than buying ready-made.
- If the free part had one dish I can't already make, like green curry from paste, so I could see the chef actually do better than a YouTube video before I sign up for anything.
- If the Sunday cook-ahead plan showed my week of food costing clearly less than eating out at 50-60 baht a plate, so the app pays for itself.
- Trying a dish I can't already make, like the green curry, and having it come out better than when I follow a clip, using just my one pan.
- Seeing in real numbers that cooking the Sunday plan for my family costs clearly less than buying from the stall all week, enough to save a lot more than 99 baht a month.
- Let me pay once, around 149 baht, to keep the dishes for good, with no monthly charge.
- Let me pay once, around 59 baht, to keep the dishes for good, with no monthly charge I have to remember to cancel.
- If I could ask the chef out loud, hands-free while I cook, instead of typing.
- If my mum or a friend I trust used it and told me the Sunday cook-ahead plan really saved them time and money, I'd try it.
- Let me pay once, like 99 baht to keep the 12 dishes, with no monthly renewal.
- If the free part let me cook something I actually get wrong from videos, like green curry, and the chef fixed my mistake while I was cooking. Then I'd see it does something a video can't.
