## What would flip a no

- If the 12-dish pack were already ready to use today, and the cook-ahead plan really fed my family cheaper than street food on busy weeks.
- Cooking two or three of the free dishes on a Sunday and having them come out right for my family, with the 40-baht-a-meal plan holding true at my market. Then I'd buy the 12-dish pack once it's actually out.
- If I cook the free dishes and the 'it didn't work' button actually saves a dinner that a video couldn't, then 99 baht for 12 more dishes ready right now, not pre-ordered, would be easy.
- If the free dishes actually got me cooking cheaper than street food every week, like the 40 baht a meal plan really working for a student alone, then 99 baht for more dishes would make sense.
- If the free dishes actually fix my mistakes on one pan and I cook them a few times, then the pack being one-pan recipes I can see first would get me to pay.
- If I cooked the free dishes a few times in my one pan and they came out better than my usual, and the 12-dish pack said which dishes work with one pan and finish in about 20 minutes.
- If after cooking the free dishes the Sunday plan really kept my meals around 40 baht and worked with one pan, then 99 baht once might pay for itself, but I'd only pay after it is released, not before.
- If I cook the free dishes a few times, they come out right, and the 40-baht-a-meal plan really beats my delivery bill, I'd pay 99 baht once the pack is actually out, not as a pre-order.
- If I use the free 5 dishes for a few weeks and the chef really fixes my mistakes better than my free AI, then I'd pay once the 12 dishes are actually there, not as a pre-order.
- If the free dishes actually saved my Sunday meal prep a few weeks in a row and the cook-ahead plan really came out around 40 baht a meal, I'd pay 99 once for more.
- If I actually cook the free dishes and they come out good, and the 12 dishes are ready to use right away, I'd pay 99 baht since it's one time and not a subscription.
- If I cook the free dishes and the 'It didn't work' button actually saves a dish a video couldn't, then I'd pay once the pack is out, not before.
