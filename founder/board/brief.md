# Board brief: ChefJiw.ai

This brief stands alone. It is everything the founder has told us.


- What it is: an AI chef ("Chef Jiw") that does all of the following in one product:
  recipes and meal plans from what is in your fridge, your diet and your budget;
  step-by-step voice/video coaching while you cook; Thai home cooking taught by
  the Chef Jiw persona; and back-of-house tools for restaurants (menu costing,
  recipe standardisation, inventory, ordering).
- Who it is for: all of these at once: busy home cooks in Thailand who cook a few
  times a week; Thai-food fans abroad who want to cook authentic Thai dishes;
  small owner-operated restaurants and cafés in Thailand; and health/diet-focused
  people tracking calories, macros or a specific diet.
- What it sells, at what price: freemium subscription: a free tier plus a paid
  plan at about 99 THB (~US$3) a month. (The price was picked from an example
  range; no separate B2B restaurant price has been set.)
- Where and how: online (web, ChefJiw.ai). Acquisition channel and delivery
  platform (web vs. mobile app) not yet decided.
- Budget and constraints: not stated. The product is not built yet: the code
  repository contains only a one-line README.
